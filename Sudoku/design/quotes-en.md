For the dialogs (PRD 3.9). Every list has 25 entries, numbered like the German
version in [quotes-de.md](quotes-de.md) (no. 7 here = no. 7 there).

- **With "— Name":** a real film line, written down from memory – **check the exact
  wording** (e.g. on Wookieepedia) before it ships.
- **Without a name:** an own parody in Star Wars style (about two thirds of the list).
- To review: cross out, change or add entries – the number per list is free, the two
  files only have to stay the same length.
